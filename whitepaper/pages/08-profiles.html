Platform Services profiles

Choose the most efficient profile that supports the workloads expected to use the region.

Enabling an additional Azure region does not require changing or duplicating the full platform footprint of an existing full-service region. An organization can maintain a small number of strategic regions providing a more complete set of Platform Services, using the Full regional platform profile, that provide resilient hybrid connectivity and common enterprise services. Other regions can use more efficient profiles, such as {{PROFILE_2}} or {{PROFILE_3}}, and consume capabilities from other more strategic regions when workload requirements permit.

These common four profiles form a spectrum: from {{PROFILE_1}}, with no dependency on another region’s hub and other platform services (although still governed from a Management Group, Policy and RBAC standpoint), through {{PROFILE_2}} and {{PROFILE_3}}, to {{PROFILE_4}}, with greater local capability and independence. They aren’t maturity stages. A profile can remain the right target for a region as long as it continues to satisfy workload and platform requirements.

Profiles describe platform services and capabilities that can be offered, not workload designs; Workload Landing Zone archetypes describe what workloads need from the platform.
