Keys: Managed HSM patterns

Keys - Patterns 4-6 use Managed HSM. Replicate Azure-held keys, or keep physical key custody outside Microsoft. EKM does not support Secure Key Release.

4Managed HSM with geo-replication
Created in
Managed HSM with multi-region replication.
Outage
Reads and writes stay available in the healthy region.
Best for
Supported shared-key dependencies with a customer-selected extended region.
Watch for
Cost. Keys, role definitions, and role assignments replicate asynchronously within 6 minutes; last changes may be lost if the primary cannot be recovered.
5Managed HSM geo-replication + external HSM / BYOK
Created in
Imported from your external HSM, then replicated.
Outage
Reads and writes stay available in the healthy region.
Best for
Same as Pattern 4, with a customer-owned external HSM as origin.
Watch for
Cost, imports, and the same replication window as Pattern 4.
6Managed HSM without geo-replication + EKM
Created in
Key never leaves your HSM.
Outage
Depends on the failing part: Managed HSM, EKM proxy, your HSM, or the region. No failover target, so wait for restore.
Best for
You must own and hold the key outside Microsoft.
Watch for
No geo-replication or end-to-end SLA. External dependency failures can interrupt workloads; verify each service integration.

Enable Managed HSM multi-region replication to one extended region. Both regions are active and Azure Traffic Manager routes to the closest one; avoid caching DNS longer than the TTL. EKM adds network round trips to your proxy and HSM on every key operation. Managed HSM does not support backup and restore of external keys: recreate the external key reference.
